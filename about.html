About Helm & Co.

We're not here to keep your seat warm.

Helm & Co. was built on one belief: marketing works best when the person who understands the brand is the one steering it. So instead of taking over, we teach.

Our story

Most agencies are built to keep you dependent. We built ours to make ourselves optional.

Helm & Co. started after one too many conversations with business owners who'd paid for "social media management" and gotten back a brand voice that wasn't theirs, a content calendar no one explained, and an invoice that never seemed to shrink.

We had a different idea: what if the agency's job was to make itself unnecessary? What if, instead of guarding the process, we handed it over — the templates, the reasoning, the shortcuts — so owners could run their own marketing with real confidence?

That's the studio we built. We still take on hands-on campaign work when it's the right fit, but our favourite outcome is a client who calls us back not because they're stuck, but because they want a second opinion before their next big move.

What we stand for

Four ideas that shape every project.

Teach, don't gatekeep

If we can explain why something works, we will — every time, not just when asked.

Your voice, not ours

We build systems around how you already talk to people, not a template voice we reuse for every client.

Plans over posts

A single post rarely matters. A system that runs for a year does. We design for the second one.

Optional, not essential

Our goal is a client who could leave anytime — and chooses to stay because the work is good.
